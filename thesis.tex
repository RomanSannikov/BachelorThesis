\chapter{Introduction}
Over the last 20 years there was a major leap in robotics, especially in the field of quadrupeds.
Many great papers developing the domain were published. And most of the researching work was done
in labs, requiring great means for prototyping and pushing the boundaries of the domain.
In this paper, I would like to review the research and practical achivements that have been done
throughout the years and combine it in a project, the cost of which will be less. The idea is to
establish a base for low-cost quadruped robots to make them more affortable for day-to-day use in
different fields of human lives.
